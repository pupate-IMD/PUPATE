Anyone can run a step

no keys, no permission

Nobody operates Pupate. Each step is a public function; a bot runs them, and if it stops, anyone else can.

Flush the tax
Moves 0.08 ETH from the hook to the vault. No reward.
Buy a seat
Fills the cheapest listing at or under 105% of the reference price. Reward 0.5% of the price. Not available: the seat pot is at 71%.
Burn
Spends up to 5% price impact of the burn pot on PUPATE and destroys it. Reward 0.5% of the ETH spent. Available in 3 blocks.
Start an auction
Opens a falling-price auction for a token the seats earned. No reward; the buyer gets the discount.

How it holds together

read in order

The token is a plain ERC-20 with 1,000,000,000 units. 85% opened the pool, 10% went to the IMD swarm that built this, 5% vests to the developer over 12 months.

Nobody can add liquidity after the block the pool opened in, so there is no way to trade around the tax by resting liquidity.

The tax can be lowered by a 48-hour timelock. Nothing can raise it. Every other knob moves only within bounds written into the contracts.

The reference price comes from the IMD oracle, which reads the collection's sales on-chain. It may rise at most 25% per 6 hours; falls are not limited. If no fresh report exists, the vault stops buying and splits the tax evenly.

Vault ETH can leave for exactly three reasons: buying a seat on Seaport, buying PUPATE to burn, or a caller reward of at most 1%.

This page describes a mechanism. It makes no statement about returns. The tax is never a surprise: the rate that applies to your trade is shown beside the button.
